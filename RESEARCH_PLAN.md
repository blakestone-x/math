# Open problems and next proof attempts

The first two targets from the previous queue that now have complete answers in their stated scope are the ten-cycle line-graph maximum and Krizek's three-prime fixed-point problem. For the Eulerian matrix square we now have a density-one root theorem at every degree and a full certificate through degree 256. Sun's prime-circle existence conjecture now has an exact answer for all integer moduli. The [scoreboard](SCOREBOARD.md) links each proof and its source.

## Next targets

| Priority | Question | First proof attempt | What would resolve this target |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | At fourteen independent cycles, is the largest line-graph signature 7 or 8? At fifteen, it is 8 or 9. [Graph reduction and exact witnesses](Line-graph-signature/forest-bounds.md) | Decompose cubic kernels at loop vertices and doubled edges. On the compressed tree, retain the boundary quadratic form with its signs. The matching bound is attained by a family of kernels, so a stronger universal estimate needs additional information from the quadratic form. | A connected simple witness attaining 8 at fourteen cycles, or a complete exclusion of signature 8 at every graph order. |
| 2 | Can a four-prime fixed point of Krizek's proper-divisor sum have three exponents equal to one? Any four-prime solution has minimum exponent at most 24, and four equal exponents have been excluded. [Classification and verifiers](A245211/three-primes.md) | Write the candidate as $p^a q r s$. Apply the subset inequalities first to the base with exponent $a$, then to the three linear local moments. Reduce the surviving residue profiles before enumerating prime intervals. | An exclusion at every exponent $a$ and all four prime bases, or an exact fixed point distinct from 21. |
| 3 | For each fixed $m$, does Wiesner's binary formula $v_2(F_{2^q-1}(2^m+1))=3q+m-3$ hold for every sufficiently large $q$? [Odd-prime proof and corrected binary rows](Franel-prime-power-rows/README.md) | Start with $m=1$. Group a row's binomial coefficients by their indices modulo two and compare adjacent rows at $2^{q-1}-1$ and $2^q-1$. The fixed-row lifting lemma already controls large exponents at each given row. | Prove the formula for all large rows at one fixed exponent, or produce an infinite sequence of row failures at a fixed exponent. |
| 4 | Does the Eulerian matrix square have only real nonpositive zeros in every degree? [Bulk and edge theorems](Eulerian-matrix-powers/README.md) | The bulk argument and the fixed-rank edge arguments each produce simple negative zeros. Derive error bounds uniform in a growing edge rank and compare them in the interval between the two proven ranges. | An all-degree proof, an infinite family with every root certified, or a degree with a certified nonreal pair. |
| 5 | Do Sun's Franel polynomials stay irreducible two and three steps past a power of two? For both offsets the eventual complementary factor can only be linear. [Dyadic bound](A000172/dyadic.pdf) · [Odd-prime digit law](A000172/digits.pdf) | Start at $2^t+2$ and $2^t+3$. Combine rational-root divisibility with the digit factorization at small odd primes and the proven negativity of every root. | Exclude all rational roots in either infinite family, or produce an exact reducible example. |
| 6 | Over which odd extension fields can one circle through all elements and have the labels $x^2+\sigma(x)$ exhaust the field? Prime fields above three and integer residue rings of order coprime to six are settled. [Moment and cycle-switching proof](Sun-polynomial-cycles/square-circle.pdf) | Begin with quadratic extensions. Classify the disconnected components whose sizes vanish in the characteristic, the precise point where the prime-field moment proof loses information. Test a second moment weighted by the field trace. | A construction for an infinite class of nonprime fields or an exact obstruction in odd characteristic. |
| 7 | Could Krizek's second divisor identity hold when the odd part has exactly two distinct prime factors with arbitrarily large exponents? The current theorem permits up to 256 divisors in the odd part. [Effective fixed-pattern theorem](A245212/prime-rays.md) | Use the monotone ray and dyadic-exponent bounds to reduce one free exponent while retaining the rational interval that determines the larger prime. Keep arbitrary odd bases throughout. | Remove the divisor-count cutoff for this class, or prove an unbounded family of exponent patterns beyond it. |
| 8 | Does a single word on a finite set of integers have nonzero Hankel determinants of every order at every shift? [Shallit's Problem 43 and three-symbol constructions](Shallit-Hankel/integer-lifts.md) | The fixed three-symbol word covers all odd orders. Locate the even-order, wrong-shift zeros in the Pagoda construction, then test a bounded change of the letters that preserves its odd-order valuation. | A single word covering every order and shift, or a proof excluding an entire fixed alphabet. |

## Further independent directions

- **Chess billiards on quadrilaterals.** For a convex quadrilateral outside the parallelogram class, derive a return map for directions near an explicitly normalized pair. A measure computation for a single such board would test how far the full-measure theorem extends. [Parallelogram proofs](Chess-billiards/README.md)
- **Queen denominators.** Close the gap between the known exponential interval and a fixed positive fraction of the Fibonacci endpoint for ordinary queens. The maximum for successive-intersection configurations is already determined. [Interval theorem](Queen-denominator-interval/README.md)
- **Gray–Fibonacci ratios.** Turn the two-limit split in each of the 140 residue classes into a recursively repeatable split. This would prove infinitely many accumulation points for either sequence. [Phase and all-seed proofs](A265387/universal.md)
- **Sigrist's binary-substring sequence.** Prove that the next term is always negligible relative to the partial sum in the limit. The prefix-crossing lemma would then imply the permutation conjecture. [Growth theorem](A316994/growth.md)
- **Binary reverse subtraction.** Bound the number of marked self-loops on a route through the width-$k$ prefix graph by $e^{o(k)}$ to obtain a subexponential stopping-time bound. [Shell proof and verification](A338203/README.md)

## Verification and publication

1. Record the original problem, hypotheses, conventions and known results. For any correction to a numbered conjecture, identify the exact parameter or formula.
2. Have an independent reader reconstruct the step on which the proof turns. For an exhaustive proof, include the reduction, coverage argument, executable checks, and expected output. State exactly which assertions Lean checks.
3. Publish editable LaTeX alongside its PDF and link the package from the scoreboard. Track new research manuscripts separately from concurrent additions to the upstream repository.
