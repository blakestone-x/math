A265386 statement review notes

Source: https://oeis.org/A265386/internal, revision #16 (January 16, 2016).
Sequence author: Stanislav Sykora (December 7, 2015).

The source defines a(1)=a(2)=1 and, for n>=3,
a(n)=gray(gray(a(n-1))+gray(a(n-2))), where gray(m)=m XOR floor(m/2).
The formal definition follows this indexing exactly. The unused a(0)=0
makes a total natural-valued function and does not affect asymptotic assertions.

The precise quantitative conjecture is the limit
(a(2n)/a(n))^(1/n) -> sqrt(2). The source also describes large and persistent
fluctuations of s(n)=a(n)/a(n-1) about that mean. The formal statement interprets
this qualitative clause as existence of a fixed epsilon>0 with ratios below
sqrt(2)-epsilon and above sqrt(2)+epsilon at arbitrarily large indices.
It does not ascribe any numerical epsilon or uniform parity bounds to OEIS.

The companion complete proof is adapted from Blake Stone's existing proof at
https://github.com/blakestone-x/math/tree/main/A265386. This contribution ports
that prior result to the site's Lean 4.33.1 / Mathlib environment and supplies
its exact target statement; it does not claim a newly discovered proof.
The additional formal corollary derives persistent fluctuations with epsilon=1/2
from the existing stronger inequalities s(2k+18)<2/3 and s(2k+17)>3, k>=0.

Only the statement is eligible for the initial formalization PR; its main body
retains the single sorry required by the site's statement-review workflow.
The separate companion proof has no unfinished obligations and must be checked
against the approved statement before it can be submitted as a proof contribution.
