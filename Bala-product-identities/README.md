# Two exponent conjectures of Peter Bala

Blake Stone · Proof developed with OpenAI Codex.

Both proposed expansions follow by separating the odd and even powers in Gauss’s triangular-number product. The proof identifies every exponent, including its coefficient, in both formal power series.
