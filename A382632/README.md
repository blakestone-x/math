# Which regular polygons can a consecutive rod chain form?

Blake Stone · Proof developed with OpenAI Codex.

The polygon conjecture in OEIS A382632 is true. Four equally spaced corners would give four triangular numbers in arithmetic progression, hence four distinct squares in arithmetic progression, contrary to the classical four-squares theorem. Chains can form a digon and an equilateral triangle.

The manuscript also constructs infinitely many chain lengths admitting at least three different equilateral foldings. It specifies three sets of corner joints in the same chain and verifies their arc lengths. The exponent-45 example has a 167-digit rod count.
